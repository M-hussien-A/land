The master plan

A community built around a creek

Inspired by the calm of water, Creek View is drawn around a winding creek that threads through 119 feddans of valleys and landscaped green corridors. Seventy percent of the land is left open — to water, to gardens, to room for life to unfold slowly.

Set directly in front of Hyde Park in the New Administrative Capital, the community places every home within reach of greenery and the city alike. Where every home looks onto something alive.

Crafted with quiet intent, for a life lived outdoors.

A home for every chapter

Lifestyle, on your terms

From a first Millennial one-bedroom to a penthouse or an I-villa with its own garden, Creek View offers a home for every chapter. Each is delivered semi-finished, leaving room to shape the interior around the way you live.

Designed for an elevated lifestyle, the community gathers its amenities along the green corridors — walking paths, shaded courtyards, and water features that make the everyday feel unhurried.

A community designed for the rhythm of real life.

The proposition

Pay nothing for six months. Then settle over fourteen years.

Your first installment is semi-annual, collected just three months after reservation — so the early months stay light. Homes are delivered within three years of signing, or two and a half years on the 6-year plan, with a payment rhythm shaped around the way life actually moves.
